# g‑magination — Brand Directions

> **Decision (Sep 2026):** direction **04 · Quiet** (pastel studio) won. The
> switcher and the other heroes were removed; the whole site now lives in the
> Quiet world. This document stays as the exploration record.

**The brief.** g‑magination is a studio that shapes light into objects — glass, glow, and the
space between. The name is a pun (g + imagination) and a promise: imagination you can hold. That
gives us an unusually wide runway. We can play it as a hushed luxury atelier *or* as a bright,
playful toy of a brand — and the wordmark survives both.

This document explores **15 directions** for the hero. Each is a complete world: its own typeface,
palette, layout, voice, and motion — not a reskin. They're grouped loosely by temperature so you
can feel the spectrum, from austere to loud. Flip through them live with the **Direction** menu
(bottom‑right); the choice persists across reloads.

A note on color: the existing site is strict monochrome with one warm accent (`#FFF2D0`, the
rope‑light). Several directions hold that discipline; others break it on purpose to show what
g‑magination looks like with a pulse. Pick the temperature first, then we tighten.

## The minimal end

### 01 · Aura — *Quiet Luxury*
The safe, beautiful default. A single oversized wordmark in heavy negative space, lit from behind
by a warm pool of light that slowly breathes. Nothing moves but the light.
- **Type** — Inter 800, tight tracking. Small‑caps overline.
- **Palette** — Ink `#0A0A0A` · Off‑white `#ECE9E4` · Rope‑light `#FFF2D0` · Smoke `#9A9A9A`
- **Layout** — Dead‑center, symmetric, generous air.
- **Motion** — Breathing aura + a barely‑there glow on the wordmark.
- **Voice** — "Light, shaped into objects. The collection begins with one."
- **Best for** — A gallery brand that wants to feel expensive by saying almost nothing.

### 03 · Spec — *Technical Studio*
Light as engineering. Left‑aligned like a spec sheet, with a metadata row. Reads like the studio
documents every object as a build.
- **Type** — Space Mono.
- **Palette** — Black `#0B0B0C` · Off‑white · **Signal lime `#C8FF00`** (labels only) · Ash
- **Layout** — Left‑aligned, data row (`ED. 001 / 001 · MONOCHROME · GLASS · GLOW`).
- **Motion** — A band of light travels through the letters.
- **Voice** — "Rendering light as matter. One object — then the file closes."
- **Best for** — A studio that wants to read as precise, modern, slightly clinical.

### 10 · Quiet — *Pastel Studio*
Minimal, but warm and human instead of black‑tie. A pale paper background, ink text, one soft
drifting shape. Boutique, friendly, calm.
- **Type** — Space Grotesk, light weight, lowercase.
- **Palette** — Blush paper `#F3E9E7` · Ink `#1A1A1A` · Dusty rose `#D8A7A0`
- **Layout** — Centered, very airy.
- **Motion** — A single soft circle floating.
- **Voice** — "Few objects. Much space. One begins the collection."
- **Best for** — A craft brand that wants to feel approachable and unintimidating.

### 13 · Grid — *Swiss Type*
International Typographic Style: a white field, a baseline grid, a big index numeral, one red
accent. Confident, classic, designer‑forward.
- **Type** — Inter, bold, ALL CAPS + tabular numerals.
- **Palette** — Paper `#FAFAF7` · Ink black · **Red `#FF3B30`**
- **Layout** — Grid lines, oversized `01`, hard left/right alignment.
- **Motion** — A rule draws itself across the baseline.
- **Voice** — "A Swiss table of light, set for one."
- **Best for** — Showing the brand is design‑literate without any decoration.

## The editorial / luxury end

### 02 · Atelier — *Editorial Maison*
Fashion‑house restraint. An elegant high‑contrast serif, an italic line, a gallery spot that
sweeps slowly across the name. French edition numbering.
- **Type** — Cormorant Garamond 300, italic for the tagline.
- **Palette** — Bistre `#14110E` · Bone `#E8E2D5` · **Antique gold `#C8A86B`**
- **Layout** — Centered, light serif at huge size, italic subhead.
- **Motion** — A shaft of warm light sweeps the screen.
- **Voice** — "Objects conceived between light and shadow — the first, and only, of its kind."
- **Best for** — Positioning g‑magination as collectible, couture, expensive.

### 11 · Cover — *Fashion Magazine*
A magazine cover, not a website. Bone stock, a top masthead rule, mixed roman/italic at display
size, a single editorial red. It looks printed.
- **Type** — Playfair Display.
- **Palette** — Bone `#ECE7DD` · Ink black · **Editorial red `#D6312A`**
- **Layout** — Masthead rule (`ISSUE 001 — LIGHT & OBJECT`), asymmetric display headline.
- **Motion** — Thin rules and lines reveal on a slow loop.
- **Voice** — "The first object, photographed in its own light."
- **Best for** — A brand that wants cultural / art‑world credibility.

### 14 · Cosmos — *Deep Field*
The mysterious one. A deep‑space field with drifting stars, a glowing orb, and a fragile serif.
Objects that hold a little starlight.
- **Type** — Instrument Serif.
- **Palette** — Space navy `#060A14` · Silver `#D6DCE6` · **Glow blue `#8FB7FF`**
- **Layout** — Centered, the wordmark floating over a starfield.
- **Motion** — Stars drift and twinkle; the orb breathes.
- **Voice** — "Objects that hold a little starlight."
- **Best for** — Leaning all the way into "mysterious," romantic and cosmic.

## The dreamy / atmospheric middle

### 04 · Vapor — *Dusk Dream*
Color enters. A dusk gradient of indigo and plum, two tinted clouds of light drifting on
independent paths, a soft serif in lilac. Ethereal.
- **Type** — Fraunces, light, lowercase.
- **Palette** — Indigo night `#0D0B1F` · Plum `#2A1B3D` · Lilac `#C9B8E6` · Warm glow `#FFD9A0`
- **Layout** — Centered, soft.
- **Motion** — Two clouds drift; lighting never repeats.
- **Voice** — "Soft light, slowly becoming an object."
- **Best for** — A brand that wants to feel like a mood, not a product.

### 12 · Liquid — *Morphing Glass*
Glass caught mid‑pour. A vivid teal→violet blob morphs and drifts behind a rounded geometric
wordmark. Modern, web‑native, a little futuristic.
- **Type** — Unbounded.
- **Palette** — Teal‑black `#04161A` · **Teal `#18C0B0` → Violet `#7A5CFF`** blob
- **Layout** — Centered over the morphing shape.
- **Motion** — Border‑radius morph + slow rotation + drift.
- **Voice** — "Glass, caught the instant before it sets."
- **Best for** — A contemporary, digital‑first identity.

## The playful / loud end

### 05 · Signal — *Neon Tube*
Industrial neon. Towering condensed all‑caps that flickers awake like a cold tube finding its
current, with a thin line drawing in underneath.
- **Type** — Bebas Neue.
- **Palette** — Black `#060606` · Off‑white · **Electric cyan `#5CF2FF`**
- **Layout** — Centered, monumental caps.
- **Motion** — Neon flicker + line draw.
- **Voice** — "LIMITED SIGNAL · ONE OBJECT · THEN DARK"
- **Best for** — Nightlife energy, drops, a brand with edge.

### 06 · Prism — *Light Refracted*
The literal idea: light split into color. The wordmark is filled with a moving spectrum that
slowly cycles hue. Playful but still sophisticated on black.
- **Type** — Space Grotesk, bold.
- **Palette** — Black `#060608` · full‑spectrum gradient text · white sub
- **Layout** — Centered.
- **Motion** — Spectrum sweeps through the letters + slow hue rotation.
- **Voice** — "Every object splits a little light differently."
- **Best for** — Saying "imagination / color / light" in one image.

### 07 · Blocks — *Bauhaus Play*
Design‑school joy. Paper background, primary red/blue/yellow shapes (circle, triangle, square)
floating behind heavy black type. Imagination assembled from simple parts.
- **Type** — Archivo Black, lowercase.
- **Palette** — Paper `#F4F1EA` · Ink · **Red `#E2402B` · Blue `#2D4EA2` · Yellow `#F2B705`**
- **Layout** — Centered type with geometric shapes drifting and rotating.
- **Motion** — Shapes float and rotate slowly.
- **Voice** — "Imagination, assembled from simple parts and primary light."
- **Best for** — A fun, confident, kid‑of‑Bauhaus brand.

### 08 · Solar — *Warm Optimism*
A small sun on a shelf. A big radial sun‑gradient (amber→coral→pink) with rotating rays and a
friendly rounded face. The happiest direction.
- **Type** — Fredoka, rounded.
- **Palette** — Sun gradient **`#FFB23E → #FF6B5E → #FF4D8D`** · Cream `#FFF7EC`
- **Layout** — Centered over the sun.
- **Motion** — Rays rotate slowly; the sun gently pulses.
- **Voice** — "A small sun you can keep on a shelf."
- **Best for** — Warm, optimistic, gift‑able, consumer‑friendly.

### 09 · Acid — *Club Flyer*
Maximum brightness. Acid green on black, monospace, with a glitch shudder and faint scanlines.
Youthful, edgy, internet.
- **Type** — Space Mono.
- **Palette** — Black `#050505` · **Acid green `#CCFF00`** · magenta glitch
- **Layout** — Centered, tight.
- **Motion** — Periodic glitch jitter + RGB split; static scanlines.
- **Voice** — "Objects rendered at maximum brightness."
- **Best for** — A rave‑flyer, gen‑z, drop‑culture energy.

### 15 · Loop — *Kinetic Marquee*
Motion as the whole idea. The name becomes two marquee strips scrolling in opposite directions,
never still. The most kinetic of the set.
- **Type** — Bebas Neue, oversized.
- **Palette** — Black · Off‑white · **Electric blue `#3D7BFF`** accent
- **Layout** — Two full‑bleed scrolling strips; a small caption centered.
- **Motion** — Continuous opposing horizontal scroll.
- **Voice** — "The name, on repeat — until you look."
- **Best for** — A bold, contemporary brand that wants to feel alive.

## How to use this

1. Open the site, click **Direction** (bottom‑right), and flip through 1–15.
2. Shortlist 2–3 by **temperature** (minimal / editorial / dreamy / playful) — not by detail yet.
3. Tell me the winner(s); I'll kill the menu, wire the chosen hero in directly, and develop it
   into a full system (type scale, color tokens, the rest of the page, the top bar).

**Note:** the fixed top bar and the sections below the hero are still the original monochrome
styling — they are *not* themed per direction yet. That's deliberate: we choose the hero world
first, then propagate it. So on the light backgrounds (07, 08, 10, 11, 13) the top‑bar wordmark
will look off — ignore it for now.
