No cookies, no analytics, no other servers

The website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising tools. It loads nothing from other servers: no external fonts, scripts, videos or maps. Everything you see comes from this website itself.

The Motion switch

If you use the Motion switch in the header, your browser stores the entry “rooms-motion” with the value “full” or “reduced” in its local storage (localStorage), so that the site keeps your choice on your next visit. The entry contains no identifier, is created only after your click and is not sent to me or to anyone else. You can delete it at any time in your browser's settings (clear site data).
